# Format: news

- **Structure:** a hook line (the finding in plain words) → 2–3 short paragraphs (what was found, how certain it is, why it matters) → CTA
- **Length:** 400–700 characters
- **Example (fictional):**
  > A tiny moon may be hiding an ocean. 🌊
  >
  > Scientists studying new telescope images think there could be liquid water under its icy crust. It's an early result, and they need more observations to confirm it.
  >
  > Why it matters: water is one of the main things we look for when searching for life.
  >
  > What would you name a new ocean world?
